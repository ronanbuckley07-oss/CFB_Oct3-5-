Inside the ML

The machine-learning win-probability model, opened up. Pick a live game or build any game state, and watch the three models (gradient-boosted trees, a logistic regression and a small neural network) work out who wins. Everything on this page is computed in your browser from the same model file the server uses.

The game state

Everything the model knows about the game, from the home team's side. Move anything and every chart below updates.

What it says

Chance the home team wins.

Why it says that

Each model's answer broken into pieces, one per input, in log-odds (the scale the models add things up on; +0.4 is roughly +10 percentage points near 50%). Blue pushes toward the home team, orange toward the away team.

The neural network, live

The inputs on the left (standardized: 0 is a typical value), two hidden layers of 16 and 8 neurons, and one output. A neuron's fill shows how strongly it's firing for this game state; a line's color shows whether that connection is pushing toward the home team (blue) or the away team (orange) right now, and its thickness shows how much. Hover a neuron for its value.
